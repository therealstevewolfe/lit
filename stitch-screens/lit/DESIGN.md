# Design System Document

This document outlines the core aesthetic and functional principles of our user interface, derived directly from our design tokens.

## Theme Overview

*   **Color Mode:** dark
*   **Seed Color:** `#9e9e9e` (A neutral gray, guiding the overall color palette.)
*   **Color Variant:** fidelity (Ensures color integrity across the design.)

## Typography

Our typography system is crafted for readability and hierarchy, utilizing distinct fonts for headlines, body text, and labels.

*   **Headlines:** `inter` - Chosen for its modern and versatile characteristics, providing clear and impactful titles.
*   **Body Text:** `inter` - Selected for its excellent readability and consistency with headline typography, ensuring a cohesive textual experience.
*   **Labels:** `beVietnamPro` - A clean and legible font specifically chosen for UI labels, buttons, and smaller text elements to maintain clarity and distinction.

## Shape and Form

The visual language of our UI elements is defined by their corner geometry and overall spaciousness.

*   **Roundedness:** `2` (Moderate rounding, offering a friendly yet professional aesthetic.)
*   **Spacing:** `2` (Normal spacing, balancing information density with visual comfort.)

## Color Palette

The primary colors define the brand's identity and interaction states, while neutral colors provide a foundation for backgrounds and text.

*   **Primary Color:** `#777777` - The dominant brand color, used for key interactive elements and primary calls to action.
*   **Secondary Color:** `#787777` - A supporting color for less prominent UI elements, chips, and secondary actions.
*   **Tertiary Color:** `#a49d9b` - An additional accent color for highlights, badges, or decorative elements.
*   **Neutral Color:** `#787776` - A neutral base color for backgrounds, surfaces, and non-chromatic elements.
