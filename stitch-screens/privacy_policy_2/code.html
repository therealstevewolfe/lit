AES-256

All stored transcripts are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 protocols. Your encryption keys are stored in the device's Secure Enclave.

Neural Engine Processing

Lit utilizes proprietary optimized Whisper models that run natively on your phone's Neural Engine. We do not use third-party APIs (like OpenAI or Google) to process your audio.

Permissions Rationale

mic

Microphone Access

Required to capture live audio for transcription. The stream is processed in real-time in RAM and is never cached or stored unless you choose to "Save Recording".

accessibility_new

Accessibility Services

Used optionally for the "Screen Record" feature. This allows Lit to capture system audio from other apps. No visual data is analyzed or transmitted.

Immediate Data Purge

Once a transcription task is completed, all intermediate temporary files—including spectral maps and tokenized chunks—are immediately purged from your device's memory using a secure overwrite method.

Analytics & Telemetry

Lit does not use third-party analytics trackers. We collect minimal, anonymized crash reports via Apple's built-in framework only if you have opted into "Share with App Developers" in your iOS/Android settings.
